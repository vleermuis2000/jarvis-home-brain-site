Jarvis Home Brain

Jarvis Home Brain is a private, self-hosted AI-powered family assistant.

About the application

Jarvis helps an authorised household organise family activities, access a shared calendar, and manage everyday information.

Google Calendar integration

Jarvis uses Google Calendar with the account owner's authorisation to retrieve events from a shared family calendar.

Calendar access is currently read-only. Jarvis cannot create, modify or delete calendar events through this integration.

Privacy

Jarvis is intended for private household use, not as a public AI service.
