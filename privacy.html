Privacy Policy

Jarvis Home Brain

Last updated: 21 October 2026

About Jarvis

Jarvis Home Brain is a private, self-hosted AI-powered family assistant intended for authorised household use.

Google Calendar data

Jarvis accesses Google Calendar data only after authorisation from the connected Google account.

The current integration uses read-only Calendar permissions to retrieve calendar information and answer scheduling questions.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to provide requested scheduling assistance. It may be processed by the configured AI and messaging services needed to deliver the assistant's responses.

Data storage

Jarvis runs on privately managed infrastructure. Authentication credentials are stored in protected local configuration and credential storage.

Conversation history and operational logs may be retained for functionality, troubleshooting and security.

Data sharing

Jarvis does not sell Google Calendar information or use it for advertising.

Access and revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: jarvis.home.family@gmail.com
